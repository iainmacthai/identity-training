Core Module Requirements Matrix

The 12 mandatory core qualifications required for offshore mobilization:

1. Identity & Maritime Travel (4 Items)

  • British Passport: 10-year validity required for all international mobilizations.
  • Driving Licence (UK): Photo ID & site vehicle mobilization.
  • Seaman's Discharge Book: Record of sea service (valid until full).
  • British Seaman's Card: Formal ILO maritime identification document.

2. Offshore Medical (1 Item)

  • OEUK Offshore Medical: Required for UKCS and global energy assets (2-year renewal cycle).

3. Survival & Escape (2 Items)

  • FOET with EBS: Further Offshore Emergency Training with Emergency Breathing System (4-year validity).
  • FOET with CA-EBS: Compressed Air Emergency Breathing System (OPITO accredited, 4-year validity).

4. Global Wind Organisation - Basic Safety (5 Items)

  • GWO Fire Awareness (FAW): 2-year renewal cycle.
  • GWO First Aid (FA): 2-year renewal cycle.
  • GWO Working at Heights (WAH): 2-year renewal cycle.
  • GWO Manual Handling (MH): 2-year renewal cycle.
  • GWO Sea Survival (SS): 2-year renewal cycle.
